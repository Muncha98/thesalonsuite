Behind-the-Chair Formula & Math Guide

1. The Developer Volume Mixing Rule

When you run short of 20 Volume (6%) developer, you can blend equal parts of 10 Volume (3%) and 30 Volume (9%) to achieve an exact 20 Vol solution. Use our Developer Mixing Calculator to get exact gram or ml measurements for any custom developer strength.

2. Booth Rent vs. 50% Commission Reality

If you generate $1,800/week in service sales on a 50% commission split, your salon takes $900 of your hard work every single week. Renting a private salon suite at $300/week + $150 in color supplies leaves you with $1,350/week — putting an extra $1,950/month in your pocket.

3. Lash Fan Safety & Natural Follicle Load

A 5D volume fan made with 0.05mm extensions exerts the identical mechanical load on a natural lash as a single 0.15mm classic extension. Always use 0.03mm extensions when building mega volume fans (7D–10D) to safeguard your clients' lash health.
