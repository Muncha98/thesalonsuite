Professional Safety Notice

  • Caution: Always perform a 48-hour client skin allergy patch test before any brow or lash tinting service — this area is close to the eye and more sensitive than scalp skin.
  • Caution: Never allow tint or developer to come into direct contact with the eye — use protective under-eye patches or petroleum jelly barrier as a precaution.

Why Brow Tint Needs Precise Developer Proportions

Brow tint cream needs a specific amount of liquid or cream oxidant to activate properly — too little developer under-processes the tint, giving a weak, uneven result, while too much can irritate sensitive brow-area skin.

Measuring tint cream by strip length (as dispensed from the tube) and converting to a precise developer drop count removes the guesswork of "a little bit" of oxidant.

🖊️ How to use this calculator

  1. Dispense your tint cream and measure the strip length.
  2. Enter the length to get the exact liquid developer drop count (or equivalent cream oxidant amount).
  3. Mix thoroughly and process 5–10 minutes, patch-testing first.
Example: a 2cm strip of tint cream needs approximately 10 drops of 10 Volume (3%) liquid developer.

Rules to Remember

  • 1. Always patch-test before full application: Brow and lash tints are applied close to the eye on sensitive skin — a 48-hour patch test is non-negotiable for new clients.
  • 2. Process time matters as much as the ratio: Even a correctly mixed tint can under- or over-deposit if left on too short or too long — stick to the 5–10 minute window and check progress visually.
