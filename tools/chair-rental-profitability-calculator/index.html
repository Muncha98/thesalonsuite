Booth Rent vs. Commission: Running the Real Numbers

Switching from commission to booth (suite) rental is one of the biggest financial decisions a stylist makes — and it is easy to get seduced by the idea of "keeping 100%" without actually running the comparison against your real weekly sales.

This calculator compares your current commission take-home against what renting a booth or suite would net you, after accounting for the fixed rent and the backbar/supplies costs you would now be covering yourself.

💈 How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your average weekly service revenue.
  2. Enter your current commission split percentage.
  3. Enter the weekly booth/suite rent you would pay as a renter.
  4. Enter your estimated weekly supply and backbar cost as a renter.
Example: $1,800/week in sales at a 50% commission split nets $900/week. The same sales as a renter paying $300 rent + $150 supplies nets $1,350/week — a $450/week gain.

Rules to Remember

  • 1. Rent is fixed — commission is proportional: On a slow week, booth rent still comes due regardless of how much you earned, while commission automatically scales down with you. Compare at your worst realistic week, not just your average.
  • 2. Renters take on real business overhead: As a renter you are now responsible for your own insurance, supplies, marketing, and often your own booking software — build these into your supply cost estimate, not just color and backbar.
