💡 Station Formula & Overview

Essential oils are highly concentrated phytochemicals. Applying them undiluted can cause chemical sensitization. Calculate exact drop counts for facial massage (0.5%–1%), full body massage (2%), or acute pain relief (3%–5%).

📐 Dilution Rules: 1 ml ≈ 20 drops. 1 fl oz (30ml) carrier @ 1% = 6 drops | @ 2% = 12 drops | @ 3% = 18 drops.
