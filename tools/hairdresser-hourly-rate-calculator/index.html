Your "True" Hourly Rate Is Lower Than You Think

Most stylists price their time based on chair-hours alone, forgetting that cleaning, restocking, consultations, and admin work are real hours spent that generate no direct revenue.

This calculator divides your net annual income by ALL hours worked — client hours plus admin hours — to reveal your true hourly rate, alongside your chair-only rate for comparison.

⏱️ How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your gross annual revenue.
  2. Enter your annual business expenses (product, rent, booking fees, etc.).
  3. Enter your billable client chair-hours per week.
  4. Enter your unpaid admin/sanitation hours per week.
  5. Enter the number of weeks you actually work per year (accounting for holiday/sick time).
Example: $65,000 revenue − $18,000 expenses = $47,000 net. At 38 total hours/week × 48 weeks = 1,824 hours/year, that is a true rate of about $25.77/hr versus a chair-only rate of $32.64/hr.

Rules to Remember

  • 1. Unpaid time is still time: If admin and cleaning hours are not counted, your real hourly rate looks artificially high — and your pricing may be too low to sustain your actual workload.
  • 2. Weeks worked matters more than people think: Vacation, sick days, and slow holiday weeks reduce your effective working weeks — use a realistic number, not 52.
