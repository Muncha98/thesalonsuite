Professional Safety Notice

  • Caution: Many keratin smoothing treatments release formaldehyde or formaldehyde-releasing agents when heat-sealed — always ensure adequate salon ventilation and check your product's safety data sheet.

⚠️ Manufacturer Specification Rule: Dosage ranges are a general guide — always follow your specific keratin brand's application amount, flat iron temperature, and wash-wait time instructions, as formulations vary significantly between brands. Mathematical calculations provide proportions, but manufacturer guidelines must always take precedence.

Dosing Keratin by Hair Length and Density

Keratin smoothing treatments need enough product to coat every strand evenly without oversaturating — too little leaves patchy results, too much can cause buildup and a greasy, weighed-down finish that doesn't smooth properly under the flat iron.

Hair length and density are the two biggest drivers of how much product a head actually needs, along with how many flat iron passes are required to properly seal it in.

💇 How to use this guide

  1. Select the client's hair length and density category.
  2. Apply thin, even layers from mid-lengths to ends first, then roots last.
  3. Flat iron with the number of passes and temperature range shown, then follow your brand's specific wash-wait time before the client showers.
Example: medium/normal hair typically needs 45–60ml of product and 4–6 flat iron passes at 400–430°F (204–221°C).

Rules to Remember

  • 1. Apply mid-lengths to ends first, roots last: This sequencing avoids over-depositing product at the scalp where it is least needed and most likely to cause buildup or irritation.
  • 2. Follow brand-specific wash-wait time exactly: Washing too soon after a keratin treatment can undo the bond before it has fully set, regardless of how well the application and flat ironing were done.
