The Physics of Safe Volume Lash Extension Geometry

A natural human eyelash follicle can only support a specific mechanical load before the dermal papilla suffers micro-trauma, leading to premature shedding, follicle atrophy, and permanent traction alopecia.

Lash weight is governed by the volume formula of a cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$. Because radius is squared, **doubling the diameter of an extension quadruples its weight**. A single 0.15mm classic lash has a mass index equivalent to approximately three 0.07mm volume extensions, six 0.05mm extensions, or seventeen 0.03mm mega volume extensions.
