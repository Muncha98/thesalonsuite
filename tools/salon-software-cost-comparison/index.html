Comparing Salon Software & Payment Processing Costs

Booking software and payment processing fees are often quoted separately, which makes it hard to see the real all-in monthly cost of different setups — a low monthly subscription with a high card fee percentage can cost more than a higher subscription with lower processing fees, depending on your volume.

This calculator estimates three illustrative fee structures against your actual monthly card processing volume, so you can see which type of pricing model tends to favor your business size.

💳 How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your estimated monthly card processing volume.
  2. Compare the three illustrative fee stacks shown in the result.
Example: at $6,000/month in card volume, a flat-fee processor, a mid-tier salon software bundle, and a premium all-in-one platform will each land at a different effective monthly cost.

Rules to Remember

  • 1. These are illustrative rates, not quotes: Real providers vary their pricing by contract, negotiated rate, and region — always get an actual quote before switching providers based on this estimate alone.
  • 2. Higher volume shifts the math toward subscription models: As card volume grows, a lower percentage fee with a higher flat subscription often becomes cheaper than a zero-subscription, higher-percentage model — recheck this calculation periodically as your volume changes.
