Budgeting a Salon Suite Launch — Startup Cost & Break-Even

Moving into your own salon suite is exciting, but the up-front costs — deposits, furniture, tools, initial product inventory — add up fast, and it is easy to underestimate how many clients you need before you are in the black.

This calculator totals your realistic startup spend and estimates both your monthly break-even client count and the total clients needed to fully recoup your initial investment.

🏠 How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your first & security rent deposit.
  2. Enter your furniture/tool build-out budget.
  3. Enter your initial color and product inventory budget.
  4. Enter your average client ticket price.
Example: $1,200 deposit + $1,500 build-out + $800 stock = $3,500 startup total. At a $95 average ticket, that is about 37 clients to fully recoup the investment.

Rules to Remember

  • 1. Startup cost is not the same as monthly cost: Your one-time build-out budget should be kept separate from your recurring monthly fixed costs (rent, software, insurance) when planning cash flow.
  • 2. Build in a cushion: New suites rarely hit full client volume immediately — budget for a ramp-up period where income may be below your break-even estimate for the first few months.
